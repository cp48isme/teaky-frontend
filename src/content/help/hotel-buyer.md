# Ordering as a hotel buyer

For the person at a hotel who orders from the hotel's ordering page. Every word in bold is a word on the screen.

## Your invitation and first sign-in

1. Open the link in the invitation email. The page says **You're invited to [the hotel]** and "Set a password for your address and you'll go straight to [the hotel]."
2. Type **Your name** ("So your printer knows who is ordering") and a **Password** ("At least 8 characters"). Press **Set password and go to [the hotel]**.
3. If your address already has an account, the page says so: press **Sign in**, or **Reset your password** if you have forgotten it.
4. "This invitation has expired.", "This invitation has already been used." and "This invitation was withdrawn." each mean you need a new link: "Ask the person who invited you to send a new one."
5. Next time, open your ordering page and press **Sign in** at the top right. On the **Sign in** card type your **Email** and **Password** and press **Sign in**. **Forgot your password?** sends a reset.
6. If you order for more than one hotel you see **Your ordering pages** — "You are signed in. Choose where to order." Press the hotel. If none is listed: "You are signed in, but your account is not attached to an ordering page yet. Contact the printer who invited you; they can add you to one."

## Around the ordering page

1. The bar at the top carries the hotel's name and **Home**, **Products**, **My Orders**, **Cart** (with a count once something is in it), then your email, **Account** and **Sign out**.
2. **Home** shows **Featured Products** and the **View Products** button.
3. **Account** is where you change your password (**Current password**, **New password**, **New password, again**, **Change password**) and see your sessions; **Back** returns you to the ordering page.
4. The foot of every page carries **Privacy**, **Terms**, **Help**, **Contact Teaky** and **Report a bug**.

## Finding a product

1. Press **Products**. If the page names a contact, it says "Need something that needs quoting, or an item not shown here? Call … or email …".
2. When there is more than one category, press a category or **All**.
3. Each card shows the product, its category, its price per pack (for example "$420.00 per 2,000") and its sizes. "No products in this category." means the chosen category is empty.
4. Press a card. **← Back to products** returns to the list.

## Choosing what to order

1. On the product page pick a **Size** and a **Color** if the product offers them. The **Price** reads per pack. If the product is sold by option (for example a locking system), press an option first — the button reads **Choose a …** until you do.
2. The page says how it is sold: "Sold in packs of 2,000. Minimum order: 1 pack." and, if there is a limit, "Up to N packs per order here. For larger runs contact …".
3. Set **Packs** (or **Qty**) with **-** and **+** or by typing. Beside it: "2 × pack of 2,000 (4,000 pcs)".
4. If the product needs a proof, the **Artwork** box asks you to choose **Reprint exactly as last time — no proof needed** or **Changes needed — a new proof will be sent before printing**. For changes, type **What changes?** and, if you have one, attach a **New artwork file (optional)** ("Artwork attached." confirms it). Unset: "If you leave this unset, a product this hotel has ordered before is treated as an unchanged reprint; a first order gets a new proof."
5. If the product is printed per person, fill **Who is each pack for?** — "Name and title as they should appear on the card, plus the email address and phone number it carries — one person per pack." Fill **Name**, **Title (optional)**, **Email** and **Phone** for every pack; the button stays off until each pack has them ("Add a name, email and phone for each pack").
6. Press **Add to Cart**. It reads **Added to Cart!** and the page says "Added to your cart: …".
7. Some products carry a **Safe Order** mark beside the name.

## Your cart

1. Press **Cart**. **Shopping Cart** lists each line with its price per pack and its pack count; change the count with **-** and **+**, or press **Remove**. The **Artwork** choice can still be changed here.
2. The totals show **Subtotal**, **Freight** ("Billed at cost on your invoice") and **Total**.
3. **Continue Shopping** goes back to the products; **Proceed to Checkout** goes on. An empty cart says "Your cart is empty" with **Browse Products**.

## Checkout

1. **Ship to** — "Filled in from your last order (…). Check it and change anything that differs." or, the first time, "Where the parcel goes and who should receive it." Fill **Hotel or business name**, **Attention (who receives it)**, **Phone** ("For the carrier"), **Address Line 1**, **Address Line 2**, **City**, **State** (two letters), **ZIP Code** and **Country**.
2. A PO Box is refused: "We cannot ship to a PO Box; every shipment goes by UPS." Outside the US: "We ship within the United States today."
3. **Freight** — "Shipped on the printer's carrier account. Billed at cost on your invoice; no freight is charged here."
4. **PO number or reference** — "Most hotels need this on their invoice. A PO number or a name both work." Some ordering pages mark it **(required)**.
5. **When do you need these by?** is optional. **Send proofs to (optional)** appears when something on the order needs a proof: "Leave blank and proofs go to …; you are copied either way." **Notes (optional)** go to the printer with the order.
6. "You will be invoiced for this order. No payment is taken now." Press **Place order — to be invoiced**.

## After you place the order

1. **Order Placed!** — "Your order … has been placed successfully." with **Status**, **Items**, **Total**, **PO number or reference** and **Your notes**.
2. **What happens next** says, item by item, whether a proof is coming ("you asked for changes, so a proof will come from … Nothing prints until it is approved.") or the item goes straight to the printer; then "You will be invoiced after it ships. No payment is taken now." and "Freight is billed at cost on that invoice; nothing was charged for it here."
3. If the order was split, each group says "N items go to the printer now." or "N items wait for your proof.", and "These groups ship and are invoiced separately, each with its own freight charge."
4. "Questions about this order?" names the contact, or says "Contact your account manager and quote …".
5. Press **View My Orders** or **Continue Shopping**.

## Proofs: what exists today

1. The printer sends a proof by email to the address under **Send proofs to** (you are copied). You approve it, or ask for changes, with the printer by email or phone. There is no approve button on the ordering page today.
2. Your order page says where it stands: "Waiting for your proof: … Nothing in this group prints until you approve it.", "Proofs approved — going to the printer.", "Proofs approved — in production.", or "Proof declined for … — a new proof will come to you."
3. Each line also reads "Proof: pending", "Proof: approved" or "Proof: rejected", and shows the artwork choice you made: "Artwork: reprint unchanged — no proof" or "Artwork: changes — new proof required".

## Following an order

1. Press **My Orders**. Each order shows its number, the date, how many items, **PO …**, what was on it, its status (Submitted, Pending approval, Approved, In production, Shipped, Delivered, Completed, Cancelled) and the total. With none: "You haven't placed any orders yet." and **Browse Products**.
2. Press an order. **← Back to orders** returns. The bar of segments fills as the order moves along.
3. The page shows **PO number or reference**, **Proofs go to**, **Your notes**, the items (**Items**, or **Group 1 of 2** "ships and is invoiced separately" when the order ships in parts), **Shipping Address** and **Order Summary** (**Subtotal**, **Freight**, **Total**).
4. Once shipped, **Tracking** shows the number and, when the carrier reports it, **Status**, **Carrier** and **Est. Delivery**.

## Ordering again

1. On **My Orders**, or on an order's page, press **Reorder** (any order that was not cancelled).
2. The items go to your cart at today's prices: "N items added to your cart at today's prices." Anything no longer sold is named: "Not added (no longer in the catalog): …". Press **View Cart** and check out as before.
